Business Interpretation
-----------------------
Intake-time attributes provide meaningful but limited predictive
power for prolonged incident resolution.

The leakage-safe model identifies approximately 64% of prolonged
incidents at intake, but its low precision produces a relatively
high number of false alerts.

Operational complexity and behavior that develops during incident
handling provide stronger retrospective risk signals.

Therefore, the recommended architecture is a layered risk
intelligence system:

1. Intake-level early warning
2. Operational complexity monitoring
3. Symptom risk intelligence
4. Combined risk escalation
5. Service recovery recommendations
